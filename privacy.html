Money Talks Daily – Privacy Policy
Last updated: 18 November 2026

Money Talks Daily ("we", "us") runs a short-form video page on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok. We use a private publishing tool ("the App") that connects to our own Instagram account through the Instagram API so we can upload and schedule our own videos.

1. Who the App is for
The App is used only by the owner of the Money Talks Daily accounts to publish content to those accounts. It is not offered to the public and no other users can sign in to it.

2. Information we access
Through the Instagram API the App accesses only our own account's basic profile information (username and account ID), the media we publish, and the links (permalinks) to those posts. We do not access, collect or store personal data about other Instagram users, followers or viewers.

3. How we use it
This information is used only to upload our videos, confirm they were published, and record their public links.

4. Storage and security
Access tokens and post links are stored only on the owner's private computer and are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

5. Data deletion
Because the App stores no data about other people, there is nothing to delete for anyone else. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will delete it within 30 days. To remove the App's access to our account, the owner revokes it in Instagram settings under Apps and Websites.

6. Contact
Send a direct message to @moneytalksdailyh on Instagram.

7. Changes
We may update this policy; the latest version will always be at this link.
